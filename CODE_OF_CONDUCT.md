# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Infinite Labs AI projects a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive, specific feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologising when appropriate, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people who use its work; and
- protecting private, sensitive, and security-related information.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- sexualised language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission; and
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing an Infinite Labs AI project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@ultima.inc**. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports as privately and impartially as practical, may request additional context, and may take corrective action appropriate to the circumstances. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

People responsible for enforcement must respect the privacy and security of reporters and affected participants.
